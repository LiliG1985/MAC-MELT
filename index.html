How ordering works

Every order is cooked fresh for your collection slot. Because we only make what's been ordered, very little goes to waste.

  1. Preorder online

    Pick your trays, pies and bowls and choose a collection slot.

  2. We cook it fresh

    Your order is made from scratch for your slot, nothing sitting under a heat lamp.

  3. Collect baked or ready to bake

    Take it hot, or prebaked to keep in the fridge or freezer and finish at home.

Made to order. Minimal waste.

Good food shouldn't end up in the bin.

We're a small home kitchen, and we cook for the orders we have, not for a shop window. That means fresher food for you and far less going to waste.

  • Cooked to order

    Every tray and pie is made fresh for your collection slot. No batch cooking, no leftovers binned at the end of the day.

  • Small batches

    We buy and prep for the week's orders, so ingredients are used up, not thrown away.

  • Bake when you're ready

    Collect ready to bake and keep it in the fridge or freezer, so you eat it when you want and nothing goes off.

Meet Raya

Raya started Mac and Melt in Sale in 2024 with one idea: take proper baked macaroni cheese and bring Caribbean flavour to it.

From weekend preorders to full event spreads, she's behind every tray, pie and bowl, and you'll often find her serving at events herself.

Our mission is to redefine macaroni cheese through refined and innovative Caribbean inspired flavours, quality ingredients, and eye catching catering that turns every event into an experience.

Raya, founder
