How ordering works

Everything is made to order, so we cook what's been ordered and very little goes to waste. It's our way of doing our bit for the environment.

  1. Pre-order online

    Pick your trays, pies and bowls and choose a collection slot.

  2. We cook it fresh

    Your order is made from scratch for your slot, nothing sitting under a heat lamp.

  3. Collect baked or ready to bake

    Take it hot, or pre-baked to keep in the fridge or freezer and finish at home.

Meet Raya

Raya started Mac and Melt in Sale in 2024 with one idea: take proper baked macaroni cheese and bring Caribbean flavour to it.

From weekend pre-orders to full event spreads, she's behind every tray, pie and bowl, and you'll often find her serving at events herself.

Our mission is to redefine macaroni cheese through refined and innovative Caribbean-inspired flavours, quality ingredients, and eye-catching catering that turns every event into an experience.

Raya, founder
