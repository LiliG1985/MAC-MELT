At home

Cooking & reheating

Everything you need to bake, store and reheat your Mac and Melt, so it tastes as good at home as it does fresh from our kitchen.

01

Cooking instructions

For unbaked trays and macaroni pies

  1. Preheat your oven to Gas Mark 5 / 200°C.
  2. Remove the tray lid and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until golden brown.
  3. From frozen, bake for 35 to 40 minutes.
  4. Serve hot for the best results. Enjoy!
02

Storage

  1. Cool completely. Let the baked mac and cheese cool to room temperature before storing.
  2. Use an airtight container. Transfer leftovers to an airtight container, or cover the tray tightly with cling film or foil.
  3. Refrigerate for up to 3 to 5 days.

Freezing for longer

  • Place in a freezer safe container, or wrap tightly in freezer safe foil or cling film.
  • Label with the date for easy reference.
  • Tip: freezing can make the pasta a little softer and may slightly dry out the sauce.
03

Reheating

Individual portions (microwave)

  1. Place a portion in a microwave safe dish.
  2. Add a splash of milk or cream to keep it moist.
  3. Cover loosely with a microwave safe lid or wrap, and heat until piping hot.

Larger portions (oven)

  1. Preheat the oven to Gas Mark 5 / 190°C.
  2. Transfer to an oven safe dish if it isn't already in one.
  3. Add a splash of milk or cream and stir gently to refresh the sauce.
  4. Cover tightly with foil to stop it drying out.
  5. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until heated through and bubbling.

Enjoy your cheesy, golden mac all over again!
